INTRODUCTION
Welcome to the Protecting Agriculture Land from Animals/Theft help center.
This system is designed to protect farmlands from intrusions by animals or theft activities. It allows farmers to monitor live video, receive alerts, and take immediate action to safeguard their property. The system uses Raspberry Pi, a camera module, and AI (YOLOv8) for real-time detection and alerts.
