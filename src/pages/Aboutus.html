Pioneering Agriculture Protection

Our advanced AI Systems is dedicated to securing farms from the wildlife and the unauthorized intrusion,ensuring crop safety and fostering harmony between humans and nature.

The Challenges

In India farming is the primary occupation and it plays a vital role for the country’s economy and culture. It involves the cultivation of crops, fisheries, horticulture and other agricultural activities. But due to the interference of animals and unauthorized persons into the farmlands leads to the economic losses for farmers and destruction of crops. To avoid these financial losses it is important to protect the farmlands from the animals.

Our Solution

To overcome this we proposed a solution in which we addressed to protect the farm land by detecting the persons and animals using cameras and provide an alert to the farmers on his mobile phone so that he is able to keep an eye on his farm when he is not around his farmland. To implement such solution we used an IoT device Raspberry Pi 4B model, cameras, speakers etc. The cameras are activated 24 hrs so that when an animal or an unauthorized person motion is detected inside the farmland it will capture the picture of the object using YOLO model and sends mail to the farmer from where he can easily access or watch the object and by using the voice modulation he can send the message to the speakers to threaten the animals and the persons. In recent research, advanced technologies such as deep learning, computer vision and intelligent hardware have been used to detect animals and unauthorized persons entering the farm and provide immediate alerts. After studying the selected research papers, their achievements are mainly highlighted as – integration of AI-powered detection systems using CNNs and YOLO models.
