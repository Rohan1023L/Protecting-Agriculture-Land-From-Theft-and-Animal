Project Name

Protecting Agriculture Land From Theft and Animal

Overview of the Project :

“ Protecting Agriculture Land From Animals/Theft” is a sophisticated web-based monitoring system that has been created to help farmers safeguard their agricultural land against unwelcome attacks in the forms of animals, birds, or human beings. The web app is set to offer real-time alerts and threat to reduce crop losses and improve agricultural yields. This project applies contemporary technologies like object detection and image processing to detect intruders in the field. After an object such as a human or animal is detected in the farm area, the system takes its picture and gives a quick alert to the farmer’s mobile phone. At the same time, a loud alarm is played through a speaker in the field to scare the intruder away. In a behavioral sense, the app has an easy-to-use and simple interface designed specifically for farmers to simplify remote monitoring of field activities. Through the provision of real-time threat detection and alerting systems, this project hopes to be a worth while contribution to contemporary farm practices , and ultimately assist farmers in safeguarding their land and crops from potential damage.
